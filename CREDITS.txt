Ito demo page: credits and licenses for the audio
=================================================

audio/ito/, audio/wav/
  Ito by Lokutor (lokutor.com). Output of the host build of the on-chip engine (bit-identical to the ESP32-S3
  firmware). CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 + Lokutor terms of use (github.com/lokutor-ai/ito/blob/main/models/TERMS.md).
  Synthetic speech in the voice of LibriTTS-R speaker 4970 (CC BY 4.0); see the repository's NOTICE.

audio/ito_g/
  Ito male voice by Lokutor, rendered the same way (host build of the on-chip engine, chip_wav.py --voice male).
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 + Lokutor terms of use. Synthetic speech in the voice of LibriTTS-R speaker 5105 (CC BY 4.0).

audio/teacher/
  StyleTTS 2 (Yinghao Aaron Li et al., github.com/yl4579/StyleTTS2, MIT License), LibriTTS pretrained model,
  conditioned on LibriTTS-R speaker 4970. Synthetic speech. Shown as the reference Ito learned from.

audio/amy/, audio/heart/
  sanoTTS by Ampixa Labs (github.com/ampixa/sanoTTS, GPL-3.0 project; MIT runtime), voices "amy" and "heart-nano",
  as publicly released. Generated by Lokutor for the same eight sentences, for side-by-side comparison only.
  Synthetic speech. Not endorsed by Ampixa Labs.

All MP3s: 24 kHz mono, 96 kbps, gain-adjusted toward -20 LUFS (no other processing).
