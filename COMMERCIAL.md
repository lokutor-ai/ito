# Commercial licensing

Ito has two parts, under two licenses:

- **The code** (engine, firmware, inference package) is free software under the **GNU GPL v3**. You may use, modify
  and ship it under those terms. For consumer devices this includes providing the corresponding source code and the
  information needed to install modified firmware.
- **The voice model that makes it talk** (`models/`) is **non-commercial**: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 plus
  [our terms of use](models/TERMS.md). The GPL on the code does not extend to the weights. Using the model or its
  audio in a product, a paid service, internal business use or commercial content needs a written license from
  Lokutor. So do weights derived from it, and commercial models trained on its output.

If you want to build something real with Ito, [Lokutor](https://lokutor.com) (Hashing Works, S.L.L., Madrid) offers:

- **Commercial licenses** for the voice model, engine and firmware, without GPL obligations, priced per device or as
  an annual license.
- **More voices and languages:** other English voices, Spanish, Catalan, Basque, Galician and more, or a voice built
  from a speaker you own or license.
- **A full on-device voice stack** for the same chip: speech-to-text ([Oído](https://github.com/lokutor-ai/oido)),
  text-to-speech and turn-taking.
- **Integration and support** on your hardware.

## Let's collaborate

We would rather see Ito used than sit on a shelf. If you are a small company, a startup, a maker selling small
batches, a school or a research group, tell us what you are building. We are happy to work something out, including
no-cost licenses. Researchers who want to go deeper are welcome too: the training code and recipe are not public, but
we do share them in research collaborations.
